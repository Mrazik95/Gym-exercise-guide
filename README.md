# Gym Guide

A pocket guide to gym exercises and training terms (AMRAP, EMOM, RPE…), in English and Czech.

Open it on your phone and add it to your home screen:
- iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen
- Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app

All content lives in `index.html`. General information only — not personal coaching or medical advice.
